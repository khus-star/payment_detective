# 🕵️ Payment Detective

### An AI-powered autonomous investigator for payment revenue loss.

> **"Revenue is down 8%. Find out why."**

Payment Detective is an agentic AI prototype that investigates unexplained payment and revenue anomalies, gathers evidence from transaction data, identifies potential root causes, challenges its own conclusions, and recommends recovery actions.

Instead of simply showing dashboards and metrics, Payment Detective tries to answer:

**What happened? Why did it happen? What evidence supports the conclusion? What should happen next?**

---

## 🎯 Razorpay Buildathon Track

**AI Revenue Recovery**

Payment failures and payment-system degradation can silently cause merchants to lose revenue.

Payment Detective is designed to detect these anomalies early, investigate their root cause, and recommend bounded recovery actions.

---

## 🚀 The Problem

A merchant notices that revenue has suddenly dropped.

Traditional analytics can show:

- Revenue decreased
- Payment success rate decreased
- UPI transactions decreased
- A particular bank has higher failures
- Latency increased

But an analyst still has to connect these signals manually.

### Payment Detective automates that investigation.

It starts with a business symptom and progressively investigates the available evidence.
